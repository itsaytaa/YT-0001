# Not Covered: brand bible

This folder is the channel's identity. Every advisory is built from it, and nothing in it changes for one episode. If something here has to change, change it on purpose: update the file, re-render the reference sheets, and log it in `CHANGELOG.md`.

| File | What it locks |
|---|---|
| `BRAND.md` | Identity, story formula, characters, the rules for new assets (this file) |
| `style.json` | Palette, fonts, safe area, caption/HUD/card/document layout, transitions, effects |
| `audio.json` | Channel voice, timing rules, drone bed, SFX, mastering |
| `voice.json` | The locked TTS model and voice, plus the voice-test history |
| `catalog.json` | Everything the shared kit can draw: shot types, elements, poses, anims, transitions, SFX |
| `scene-library.md` | What each element, pose and shot type looks like, in words |
| `reference/*.png` | Golden images of the characters, props, layout and cards. These *are* the look. |
| `sfx/` | The channel's sound effects (the drone bed is regenerated by `scripts/sfx.mjs`) |
| `templates/new-advisory/` | Starting files for the next advisory |

## 1. Identity

- **Channel:** Not Covered (the things the news never covers).
- **Narrator:** the **Department of Unlisted Risks** (DUR), a fictional federal department with a quiet, FBI-like manner. It is not an insurer and it sells nothing. It calls itself "the Department" or "we", never anything else.
- **Language:** English only, in the script, captions, on-screen text, metadata and thumbnails.
- **Voice:** low, calm, adult male, deadpan. A civil servant reading a routine advisory. The narration uses **MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD**, voice **`English_ManWithDeepVoice`**, through OpenRouter as **`minimax/speech-2.8-hd`**. It is locked (see `voice.json`): the same narrator every episode. MiniMax ignores tone instructions, so delivery comes from punctuation and line breaks; write for it (short sentences, ellipses for held pauses).
- **Episode unit:** an **Advisory** with a four-digit number (`0001`, `0002`…). The title is a YouTube hook ("If Your Cat Ever Stares At An Empty Corner… DO NOT LOOK"); the video itself always calls it "Advisory 000X".
- **Tone:** procedural dread. The Department is never surprised, never jokes, and never explains more than it must. Horror comes from the calm.
- **Footer line** (description): *This channel is fiction. The Department of Unlisted Risks does not exist. Probably.*

## 2. The advisory formula

Every advisory has the same skeleton, so viewers learn the ritual. The beat ids are fixed (`catalog.json → beats`).

| Beat | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `cold_open` | 3–5 lines | An ordinary household moment that is slightly wrong. Ends with "This is Advisory 000X." |
| `teaser` | 2–3 lines | What the advisory covers, plus the promise: "There are 6 directives. Follow all of them." |
| `world` | 4–6 lines | The rules of this risk: what it is, how long the Department has known, why it is unlisted. A document or case file appears here. |
| `directive_1` … `directive_6` | 5–9 lines each | Card, then the rule, then the reason, then the consequence. Each directive escalates. |
| `addendum` | 6–10 lines | The reveal: what is really going on. The inspector (or the episode's equivalent) is seen clearly for the first and only time. |
| `callback` | 2–4 lines | Returns to the cold open with new meaning. |
| `outro` | 2–3 lines | The sign-off, then the end card. |

**Budget:** 600–760 spoken words, about 4:30–5:30 at the channel voice's pace. Exactly six directives, then one addendum.

**Recurring devices**, at least three per episode:
- **Refusal:** *"We will not be putting that in writing."*
- **The slip:** once per episode, in a directive between 4 and 6, the narrator says something too personal ("we", "your book", "we watched"). The next line is *"Apologies. That remark has been redacted."* Its visual is the grey frame plus the `slip` transition. List the slip line ids in `scenes.json → meta.slipLines` so they get the warmer, quieter treatment in post.
- **Reveal line:** *"The Department of Unlisted Risks has [done something] for [N] years."*
- **Sign-off:** *"This advisory has ended."*, then the end card stamp (one status word: ONGOING, CONTAINED, PENDING, UNRESOLVED, LISTED…) and "NEXT ADVISORY: 000X".

**Writing rules**
- One line is one caption is one shot. A caption is at most 95 characters (two lines on screen).
- In `voice`, spell numbers out ("eleven years"). In `caption`, use digits ("11 years"). Every `emphasis` entry must appear word for word in the caption (`validate.mjs` checks this).
- Directive titles are imperatives, ≤ 6 words where possible: "Do not look", "Do not say hello".
- The viewer is "you". Humans are never named.

## 3. Characters

All characters are drawn by the kit. **Use them as they are**: do not redraw them, restyle them or draw new versions for an episode. See `reference/characters.png`.

| Character | Look | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Person ("you")** | Solid fg pictogram. **Rounded-square head**, no face, rounded-rect torso, stroke limbs. 9 poses, 5 facings. | The viewer's stand-in, in every advisory. Can be `dim`/`grey`, or `outline: red` when in danger. Standard height 360 px in a wide shot. |
| **Inspector** | Grey, faceless, brimmed hat, long coat. 4 poses (stand, corner, lean-over, bust). | The Department's recurring "unlisted" presence. Only ever a hint (low opacity, edge of frame, behind something) until the addendum. Never moves fast, never touches anyone. Can be the risk itself (0001) or a Department agent; never both in one episode. |
| **Cat** | Solid fg cat, 9 poses, can be old (squinting eyes, slower). | Recurring household witness. Optional in future advisories; when it appears it is the *same* cat. |
| **Crowd** | Rows of small people. | Statistics, "households", "occupants". |

**Household props** (`reference/props.png`): sofa, armchair, bed, floor lamp, window (moon/sun/day), bookcase, clock, door, mat, box, book, cat bed, bird, mini document. **Annotation set:** corner, gaze line, mark (cross/check/circle), eye, label, speech, thought, boundary, glow, checklist, counter, isometric room.

**New characters** are allowed only when the risk needs one (a neighbour, a child's drawing, the thing in the attic). Build it with the rules in §5 and keep it in the episode folder. When a second advisory uses it, it gets promoted into the kit (§6).

## 4. Layout (locked)

See `reference/layout.png` and `reference/cards.png`. Values live in `style.json`.

- **Canvas:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, background `bg #0B0D10`, film grain, a slow vignette.
- **HUD** (top 100 px): `DEPT. OF UNLISTED RISKS / ADVISORY {advisory}` at top left, a REC dot and timecode at top right, a directive chip once a directive starts. HUD text comes from `style.json → hud` with `{advisory}` filled in.
- **Scene zone:** y 150–820. The **ground line is y = 780**. Figures stand on it.
- **Caption zone:** y 860–1000, centred, IBM Plex Sans Condensed 54 px, sentence case, emphasis in blue (or red when the shot's accent is red). Nothing in the scene may enter it; `validate.mjs` warns when a figure is placed at y > 830.
- **Directive card:** "ADVISORY 000X · DIRECTIVE", an outlined two-digit number (Archivo 800, width 75), the title in caps, a blue rule drawing on, then the stamp sfx.
- **Addendum card:** the same, in red.
- **Documents:** off-white paper (`paper`/`ink`), redaction bars, a red rubber stamp. Documents are the only light surface in the video.
- **End card:** a red stamp with the status word, then "NEXT ADVISORY: 000X".
- **Transitions:** `cut` (default), `hold` (same shot, new caption), `scan` (photocopier bar, into documents), `carbon` (blue offset ghost), `slip` (the slip only), `fade` (opening and end only).
- **Colour meaning:** blue = the Department, allowed, annotation. Red = forbidden, danger, stamps. Grey = the inspector, and the whole frame during the slip. Never add a fourth accent colour.

**Composition lessons from Advisory 0001** (apply them from 0002 on):
- Small figures in a big empty frame read as "lonely" once; after that they read as "empty". The hero figure should fill at least a third of the frame height (person ≥ 360 px), and close-ups should use `push` or the close-up poses.
- Close-up corners and inspectors belong at y ≤ 815, not 860 (0001 needed the `liftCloseUps` patch for this).
- When an emphasis word never highlights it is almost always a mismatch between `caption` and `emphasis` (0001 had "six directives" against "6 directives"). The validator now catches this.

## 5. Making a new asset

New content can and should create assets for its own features: a radiator, a basement stair, a doorbell camera feed. Make them look like they were always part of the kit:

1. **Palette tokens only:** `C.fg`, `C.dim`, `C.line`, `C.grey`, `C.blue`, `C.red`, `C.paper`, `C.ink`, `C.surface`, `C.surface2` from `video/src/theme.ts`. No new hex values.
2. **Figures** (anything alive, or anything the story is *about*) are solid `fg` shapes with round caps and joins and the `GLOW` filter. No faces, no eyes on humans. An eye is drawn only as the `eye` annotation.
3. **Furniture and set dressing** is drawn as an outline on a surface: a `C.surface`/`C.surface2` fill with a `C.line` or `C.dim` stroke of 2–3 px. No glow, so the figures stay the brightest things on screen.
4. **Annotations** are 3 px strokes in blue or red; labels use IBM Plex Mono 24 px, letter-spaced, with a thin leader line.
5. **Scale:** stand it on the ground line next to a 360 px person and check that the proportions look like the reference sheet.
6. **Motion:** reuse the kit's anim vocabulary (`breathe`, `pop-in`, `slide`, `draw-on`, `fade-out`, `tick`…). Nothing bounces, nothing is fast except `spin-fast`, and nothing has easing overshoot.
7. **Text on an asset** is English, uppercase mono, and short.

## 6. Reuse first, then episode assets, then promotion

1. **Reuse first.** Check `catalog.json` and the reference sheets before drawing anything. Most scenes are a new arrangement of existing elements. Positions, poses, opacity, `color`, `outline` and anims cover a lot.
2. **Episode assets.** Something the kit doesn't have and only this advisory needs:
   - declare its name in `scenes.json → meta.customElements` (for a tableau element) or `meta.customShots` (for a whole new shot type);
   - implement it in `video/src/episodes/<slug>/assets.tsx`, following §5;
   - use it in shots like any other element (`{ "el": "radiator", "x": 1300, "y": 780, "h": 300 }`).
   The validator fails if a declared asset isn't registered, and warns if its name clashes with a kit asset.
3. **Promotion.** When a second advisory needs the same asset, move it into the kit (`video/src/figures/` or `video/src/shots/`), add it to `catalog.json` and `scene-library.md`, add it to the right reference sheet, run `node scripts/check-reference.mjs --update`, bump `kitVersion`, and log it in `CHANGELOG.md`. Earlier episodes keep working because they now pick it up from the kit.

**Never edit kit files for one episode.** Episode corrections go in `video/src/episodes/<slug>/patches.ts`. A kit change that is meant to change the look goes through the reference check, and every earlier advisory should still render the same.

## 7. Thumbnails and metadata

- **Thumbnail:** near-black, one big pictogram moment (a figure plus the threat), 2–4 words in Archivo 800 caps with one word in red, and a small "ADVISORY 000X" tag. No faces, no photos, no arrows. Each episode has its own `Thumbnail.tsx` in its episode folder; copy 0001's and change the scene.
- **Title:** "If Your [ordinary thing] Ever [does something]… DO NOT [verb]" or a similar imperative hook. ≤ 70 characters.
- **Description:** two short hook lines in the Department's voice, "Advisory 000X · Classification: unlisted", chapters (one per beat group: opening, each directive, addendum), the fiction footer, then three hashtags.
- **Tags:** the topic phrases first, then the fixed set: `analog horror, rules horror, creepy instructions, scary rules, government advisory horror, animated horror, pictogram horror, not covered, department of unlisted risks`.
